## Inspiration & Credits

The historical HTML editing workflow of [Y[wai] / waiVSCode](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=corernd.wai-vscode)
and the user's [waiVSCode manual](https://uitool.org/uitool/file/waiVSCode_manual(en).pdf)
inspired the product brief. Modern workflow references include
[Codux](https://www.codux.com/), [Onlook](https://github.com/onlook-dev/onlook),
[Puck](https://github.com/puckeditor/puck), [Storybook](https://storybook.js.org/),
[React Grab](https://github.com/aidenybai/react-grab), [Craft.js](https://github.com/prevwong/craft.js),
[SculptUI](https://github.com/sculptui/vscode), and [Plasmic](https://github.com/plasmicapp/plasmic).
[ts-morph](https://github.com/dsherret/ts-morph), [Recast](https://github.com/benjamn/recast),
and [dnd-kit](https://github.com/clauderic/dnd-kit) informed the design brief.

These are independent projects, not sponsors or affiliates. Their product names and
